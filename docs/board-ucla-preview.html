      Project Crosscheck

      A public, side-by-side table of NHTSA and IIHS safety ratings for model years 2023–2026, built into the VINdicated site.

      Question for v1: how often do NHTSA and IIHS ratings disagree? Which one better predicts real-world outcomes is saved for v2.

      Data: NHTSA ratings are public. IIHS needs an approved API key; the application is in.

      Rules: no combined safety score, no scraping iihs.org, and the public table is generated by the pipeline, never edited by hand.

      Research projects

      Language, Trust & Access: how multilingual consumers experience communication, trust and access to information at dealerships.

      The Hidden Price Gap: what makes car pricing hard to understand: dealer add-ons, fees, "starting at" prices and monthly payment ads.

      After the Accident: Know Your Rights: a plain-language California guide for when something goes wrong: accidents, dealer disputes, fraud, repair and financing problems.
