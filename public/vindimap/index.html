The VINdisclaimer.

Read this before you open the map. It explains what the map is, how we made it, and what it can't tell you.

01

This is a summary of opinions, not a verdict.

Everything on this map comes from public reviews written by other people. The tiers, categories, and quotes describe what those reviewers said. They are not VINdicated's findings, accusations, or statements of fact about any business, and they do not mean a dealership broke the law or did anything wrong.

02

The sample is small.

We read up to five public Google reviews per dealership, the ones Google made available when we collected the data, for 100 dealerships on LA's Westside. A dealership may have hundreds of reviews we didn't read. Five reviews can't tell you how a business treats most customers.

03

An AI model sorted the reviews, checked against human labels.

Reviews were classified by a language model following strict written rules. Our team hand-labeled a set of reviews (a “gold” set) and tested the model against it to confirm it sorts complaints the way a careful person would. No model is perfect: it can still misread sarcasm or context, or put a complaint in the wrong category, and not every individual result on the map was re-checked by hand.

04

We can't verify what reviewers say.

We don't know who wrote each review or whether it's accurate. Reviews can be mistaken, exaggerated, outdated, about a problem the dealership later fixed, or fake. We haven't contacted the dealerships or the reviewers.

05

It's a snapshot in time.

The data was collected in September 2026. Staff, ownership, and practices change, and so do reviews. A dealership's tier may not reflect how it operates today.

06

“Clear” isn't an endorsement. “Flagged” isn't a warning to stay away.

A clear dealership can still treat you badly, and a flagged one may treat you well. Use the map as one reason to ask more questions, never as your only source.

07

Not legal, financial, or professional advice.

Nothing here is advice about any purchase, contract, or dispute. Always read your paperwork, get an independent pre-purchase inspection, and talk to a qualified professional about your situation.

08

Protecting people's privacy.

We removed employees' names from the quotes we show. Complaints are about how a business was described, not about any individual.

09

No affiliation.

VINdicated is an independent student nonprofit. We're not affiliated with, paid by, or endorsed by any dealership, manufacturer, or Google. Business names and trademarks belong to their owners.

10

Provided as is.

We work to keep this accurate but make no guarantees about its completeness or accuracy, and we're not responsible for decisions made based on it.

11

Something wrong? Tell us.

If you represent a dealership, or you think a review was misread, contact us at getvindicated@outlook.com with the dealership's name and what you believe is inaccurate. We'll review it and correct or remove information that's wrong.

How we made it.

  1. 1

    Collect

    We gathered public Google Maps listings for 100 dealerships and service centers in Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, West LA, Culver City, and West Hollywood, with up to five public reviews each.

  2. 2

    Classify

    A language model, tested against reviews our team labeled by hand, read each review and sorted complaints into eight categories. It could only flag a complaint by quoting the reviewer's exact words, about that dealership, that wasn't already resolved. Minor gripes, speculation, and general negativity don't count.

  3. 3

    Tier

    Clear: no review contained a qualifying complaint. Caution: at least one review contained a complaint. Flagged: at least one review described several different kinds of problems at once.

  4. 4

    Show

    We display the tier, how many of the reviews we read were flagged, one short quote per concern with staff names removed, and a link so you can read every review yourself on Google.

The eight complaint categories

Our classifier only counts a complaint if it can point to the reviewer's own words, about this dealership, that wasn't already made right. Minor gripes don't count.

  1. Price & fee surprisesCharged more than advertised or agreed: hidden fees, last-minute markups, unauthorized add-ons or repairs.
  2. Vehicle misrepresentedThe car's condition, history, or availability wasn't what they said, or a repair wasn't really fixed.
  3. Contract & document issuesPaperwork that was confusing, altered, or withheld, like unclear loan terms or a delayed title.
  4. Pressure & manipulationAggressive or dishonest tactics to rush or force a decision.
  5. Post-sale neglectAfter the sale or service, the dealer went quiet or didn't keep its promises.
  6. Discriminatory treatmentThe reviewer says they were treated worse because of who they are.
  7. Poor serviceRude, dismissive, or unprofessional staff, or long unexplained delays.
  8. Other complaintA specific, real complaint that doesn't fit the categories above.

Classifier version 1.5.0, validated against our hand-labeled gold set and run September 24, 2026. Tier rules are applied automatically to the classified reviews.
